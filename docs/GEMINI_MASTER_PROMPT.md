## THE VOCABULARY CRISIS (the emotional entry point)
A study of 7 Mongolian daily newspapers (total word count in one day's editions), after removing duplicated words and proper nouns (personal names, place names), found only **2,944 unique words** remain. State documents cycle through approximately **300 words**. Modern Mongolian has become extremely shallow on philosophical, religious, and expressive vocabulary — not because it is obsolete, but because no one has made it findable.

Meanwhile the Ganjuur 108 volumes are the single deepest, most logically structured Mongolian ever written. It is the carrier-class corpus that could restore that depth. Right now it sits in archives and private collections without a canonical digital form.
